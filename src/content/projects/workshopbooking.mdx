## Overview

Workshopbooking is a booking-centred platform concept built around clarity and ease of movement. The design prioritises a predictable layout, obvious next steps, and a reservation flow that removes as much friction as possible.

## Approach

The experience starts at the information architecture — every page is mapped around what the user needs to decide next. Wireframes were tested and iterated on before the visual layer, keeping the structure legible even before styling came in.
