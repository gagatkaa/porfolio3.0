## Overview

Soundwaveclock is an experimental web project where time stops being a number and becomes a visual, rhythmic field. Hours, minutes, and seconds drive an audio-reactive visualisation — the clock is read through motion and sound as much as through digits.

## Approach

The interface combines Web Audio synthesis with generative graphics. Each time unit maps onto a layer of the composition, so as the clock ticks, the image and its accompanying tone evolve subtly. The design leaves room for the output to be abstract, inviting more of a felt sense of time.
