## Overview

A Kickstarter-focused project applying design to the mechanics of crowdfunding: getting people to understand a product, believe in it, and back it within seconds. The work covers the campaign identity, the pitch narrative, and the supporting assets needed across tiers, updates, and social channels.

## Approach

The design leans on storytelling rather than decoration — every visual element has a job in the pitch. Strong hero moments, clear benefit communication, and a consistent identity across the campaign page and media kept the message easy to follow at every stage.
