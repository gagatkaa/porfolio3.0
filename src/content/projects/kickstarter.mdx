## Overview

**AlphalyteBlend** was a motion-design project focused on improving an existing Kickstarter campaign for a lightweight, collapsible portable blender.

The original campaign struggled to communicate the product clearly and lacked the energy expected from a short promotional video.

My goal was to rebuild the presentation around a stronger visual system, clearer messaging and more dynamic motion.

<Image src={mainProduct} alt="AlphalyteBlend campaign animation" />

</div>

<div class="case-section">

## The challenge

The product had several clear selling points:

- collapsible and lightweight
- portable
- powerful enough for smoothies and ice
- USB-C rechargeable
- reusable and easy to carry

The challenge was turning those features into a short video that could be understood almost immediately.

Rather than explaining everything in detail, I focused on **one message per scene** and created a faster, more structured rhythm for the campaign.

</div>

<div class="case-section">

## Visual direction

I developed a bright and playful visual language inspired by **fruit, geometric forms and bold color blocking**.

The styleboard helped define the palette, shapes and overall energy before moving into animation.

<Image src={closeup1} alt="AlphalyteBlend visual direction" />

The strong colors and simple compositions were chosen to make the product feel lightweight, fresh and energetic while keeping the blender as the main focus.

</div>

<div class="case-section">

## Story and motion

Before animating, I rewrote the campaign into a short sequence built around the product's key benefits.

The animation moves from introducing the blender to showing its portability, power, charging and everyday use, finishing with a direct Kickstarter call to action.

<Image src={closeup2} alt="AlphalyteBlend script" />

I used **After Effects** to create the motion, combining product movement, typography and graphic transitions to keep the pacing fast and visually consistent.

</div>

<div class="case-section">

## Outcome

The final animation turns a weak product pitch into a more focused and energetic Kickstarter presentation.

<Image src={closeup3} alt="AlphalyteBlend final result" />

For me, the project was an exercise in using **motion design as communication** — deciding what information matters, how quickly it should appear and how visual rhythm can make a product easier to understand.

</div>
