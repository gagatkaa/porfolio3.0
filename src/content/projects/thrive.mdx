## Overview

Thrive is a brand identity concept built around growth and wellbeing. The visual direction favours calm, breathing space, and a confident palette — a system designed to feel reassuring rather than loud, while still carrying enough personality to be memorable.

## Visual system

Typography and space do most of the work. The identity includes a considered logomark, a flexible colour and type system, and motion that reinforces the idea of steady, gentle growth across digital and physical touchpoints.
