## Overview

**SoundWaveClock.exe** is a small creative-coding experiment that explores what happens when a familiar object — a digital clock — becomes something more fluid and interactive.

Instead of treating time as static numbers, the project combines the clock with adjustable visual parameters, allowing its appearance and movement to be manipulated in real time.

<Callout type="key" title="key fact">
  A clock you can play with — time becomes an **interactive visual system** instead of static numbers.
</Callout>

</div>

<div class="case-section">

## Shaping time

The interface exposes a small set of controls for **amplitude, period and weight**, alongside direct control over the displayed time.

Changing those values transforms the behaviour of the visual system, turning the clock into something that can be played with rather than simply read.

A play control adds another layer of movement, making the piece feel closer to an instrument or visual experiment than a conventional clock.

</div>

<div class="case-section">

## Interaction

I intentionally kept the interface minimal.

Rather than explaining every parameter, the project encourages experimentation: move a value, observe how the system reacts, and gradually understand the relationship between the controls and the resulting form.

</div>

<div class="case-section">

## Outcome

SoundWaveClock.exe was an experiment in combining **time, motion and user-controlled parameters** inside a simple web interface.

It helped me explore how even a very familiar piece of information can become expressive when its visual rules are exposed to the user.
