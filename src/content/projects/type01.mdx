## Overview

Type01 is a typographic exploration where letterforms do all the talking. The project strips the design back to a single type family and asks how far one voice can carry — structure, hierarchy, and identity generated purely through type choice, scale, and spacing.

## Approach

Work began with specimen-level studies of the letterforms before moving into layout exercises. The result is a small but complete typographic system — a toolkit of rules, grids, and treatments that can shape everything from a poster to a small identity.
