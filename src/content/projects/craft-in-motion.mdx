## Overview

Craft in Motion is a web project built around making and creative practice. The site treats motion as the primary way of telling the story — elements move with purpose, guiding the viewer through the work rather than merely decorating it.

## Approach

Animation was planned from the start: scroll timelines, sequencing, and easing were designed alongside the layout so the two feel inseparable. The result is a browsing experience that feels alive and intentional, with performance kept in mind so the motion never gets in the way of the content.
