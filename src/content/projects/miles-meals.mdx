## Overview

Miles & Meals is a brand identity project exploring the overlap between two loves — travel and eating well. The visual system aims to feel warm and human: editorial layouts, considered type, and colour that evokes both packed bags and home-cooked comfort.

## Visual system

The identity is built to stay flexible across touchpoints — from printed food maps to social storytelling. Type and photography carry most of the tone, with motion used sparingly to keep the brand grounded rather than overproduced.
