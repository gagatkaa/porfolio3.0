## Overview

**Miles & Meals** is a food-box concept built around the idea of experiencing a city through its food.

Instead of positioning the service as another meal kit, I designed it as a **travel-driven food experience**: each box represents a destination and brings local street-food recipes, ingredients and stories into the user's kitchen.

The app connects the full experience — from receiving and managing food boxes to cooking recipes and discovering the cities they come from.

## The concept

The core idea was:

**Street food. Local stories. At home.**

Each delivery contains recipes and pre-portioned ingredients inspired by a specific place, with different box types built around local food culture:

- **Local Legend** — iconic dishes people grow up with
- **Street Made** — bold, fast and flavour-heavy street food
- **Neighbourhood Bakes** — local snacks, pastries and baked treats

The goal was to make discovering new cuisines feel accessible while keeping the sense of place and story behind the food.

</div>

<div class="case-section">

## App experience

The app was designed around the complete customer journey.

Users can manage upcoming food-box deliveries, browse the recipes included in each box, follow cooking instructions and explore the destinations behind the dishes.

A recipe page combines practical cooking information — preparation time, servings, ingredients and instructions — with the story and location behind the dish.

The **Explore** section expands the experience beyond the food box itself, allowing users to discover cities, local dishes and new destinations.

</div>

<div class="case-section">

## Brand identity

The visual identity is based on the idea of **travel translated into a collectible system**.

Rather than relying on literal travel imagery, I used elements inspired by:

- tickets
- luggage labels
- destination stickers
- navigation symbols
- travel stamps

These are combined with warmer, more expressive food-inspired colors and shapes.

The result balances two sides of the brand:

**structured travel systems + expressive food culture**

This helped create an identity that feels playful and adventurous without becoming visually chaotic.

## Visual system

The interface uses bold colors, rounded shapes and modular cards to create a friendly and accessible experience.

Travel-inspired labels and icons help communicate movement and destination, while food photography and warmer tones keep the focus on flavour and discovery.

<Image src={styleboard} alt="Miles & Meals styleboard" />

The same system was designed to extend beyond the app into packaging and physical food boxes, making each delivery feel like an object that has arrived from somewhere rather than a generic meal kit.

</div>

<div class="case-section">

## Outcome

Miles & Meals became a complete brand and digital product concept connecting **food, travel and discovery**.

The project allowed me to work across brand identity, UX structure and interface design while keeping one central idea consistent across the entire experience:

**every box is a small journey to another city.**
